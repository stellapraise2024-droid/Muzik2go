# **MUZIK2GO**

## **Product Requirements Document**

**Product:** Muzik2Go  
**Category:** AI-powered music creation, vocal development and artist studio  
**Platforms:** Mobile \+ Web  
**Product positioning:** **Your Studio, Anywhere.**  
**Core philosophy:** **Create. Practice. Record. Improve. Evolve.**

---

# **1\. PRODUCT VISION**

Muzik2Go is a digital music studio designed to give singers and artists the ability to develop themselves and their music without depending entirely on a physical recording studio.

Muzik2Go combines:

* Vocal practice  
* AI vocal coaching  
* Recording  
* Vocal analysis  
* Songwriting  
* Melody development  
* Music/instrumental development  
* Song production assistance  
* Recording improvement  
* Polishing and mastering  
* Collaboration  
* Artist development  
* Learning  
* Community feedback  
* Artist identity development

The product should feel less like a collection of music tools and more like a **personal studio that grows with the artist**.

---

# **2\. THE CORE PROMISE**

> **Bring whatever you have. Muzik2Go helps you take it further.**

An artist can enter Muzik2Go with:

* Nothing but an idea  
* A melody  
* A hummed recording  
* Lyrics  
* A beat  
* An instrumental  
* An unfinished song  
* A rough demo  
* A completed recording

The studio should meet the artist where they are.

---

# **3\. TARGET USERS**

Muzik2Go serves artists at different stages.

## **Beginner**

Someone who wants to learn how to sing, practice, develop confidence and create their first songs.

## **Developing Singer**

Someone who already sings but wants structured practice, feedback, analysis and improvement.

## **Independent Artist**

Someone creating songs without having constant access to producers, engineers or physical studios.

## **Professional Artist**

Someone who already understands music production but wants a convenient private environment for developing ideas, rehearsing, recording demos and experimenting.

## **Songwriter**

Someone who may not primarily identify as a singer but wants help developing lyrics, melodies and song structures.

---

# **4\. PRODUCT PRINCIPLE**

Muzik2Go follows one central rule:

> **The artist remains the artist. AI is the assistant.**

The AI can suggest, analyze, coach, develop, experiment and improve.

The artist decides what stays.

The artist decides what changes.

The artist decides what the final song becomes.

# **8\. MY VOICE**

"My Voice" is the artist's personal vocal profile.

It should develop over time.

It can contain:

* Vocal range  
* Comfortable range  
* Developing areas  
* Pitch development  
* Timing development  
* Vocal control  
* Breath control  
* Strengths  
* Recurring challenges  
* Performance characteristics  
* Practice history  
* Vocal progress

The AI should use previous sessions to make future coaching more personal.

Example:

> "You've become more consistent in this part of your range compared with your earlier recordings."

---

# **9\. AI VOCAL COACH**

The AI should listen to the singer and help them understand their performance.

It can identify areas such as:

* Pitch  
* Timing  
* Rhythm  
* Vocal consistency  
* Breath control  
* Vocal control  
* Delivery  
* Strengths  
* Areas requiring practice

The AI should explain **what happened and how to improve**, rather than simply producing a score.

Instead of:

> "72/100"

the experience should favor:

> "Your pitch was stable through most of the chorus. The final note of the second line dropped slightly. Let's practice that transition."

The artist can immediately turn an identified weakness into a practice exercise.

---

# **10\. SONG ANALYSIS**

Muzik2Go should analyze the song as well as the singer.

The AI can examine:

* Lyrics  
* Melody  
* Song structure  
* Repetition  
* Arrangement  
* Emotional direction  
* Vocal delivery  
* Relationship between vocals and music

The goal is to help the artist understand the complete work.

Example:

> "Your chorus has a strong melodic identity, but the verse doesn't create enough contrast before it."

The artist can then choose whether to act on the suggestion.

---

# **11\. AI MULTI-ROLE ASSISTANT**

Muzik2Go should have one central AI that can operate in different roles.

## **AI Vocal Coach**

Helps the artist practice and improve their voice.

## **AI Producer**

Helps develop musical direction, arrangement and song ideas.

## **AI Songwriter**

Helps develop lyrics, melodies, concepts and song structure.

## **AI Studio Engineer**

Helps analyze and improve recordings.

## **AI Creative Partner**

Helps artists experiment and overcome creative blocks.

The artist can explicitly request a role or simply describe what they need.

Examples:

> "Coach me."

> "Help me finish this chorus."

> "Why doesn't this vocal sound right?"

> "Give me three directions for this song."

* Alternative melodies

# **15\. RECORDING STUDIO**

The recording experience should support:

* Acapella recording  
* Recording over instrumentals  
* Multiple takes  
* Different song sections  
* Lead vocals  
* Harmonies  
* Doubles  
* Ad-libs

The artist should be able to keep several attempts rather than feeling forced to overwrite previous performances.

---

# **16\. TAKE MANAGEMENT**

Muzik2Go should make repeated recording useful.

Artists can:

* Record multiple takes  
* Listen to takes individually  
* Compare takes  
* Mark favorites  
* Keep older versions  
* Identify strongest performances  
* Build a final performance from selected sections

For example:

> Verse — Take 2  
> Chorus — Take 4  
> Bridge — Take 3  
> Ad-libs — Take 5

AI can provide observations, but the artist makes the final selection.

---

# **17\. RECORDING IMPROVEMENT**

After recording, the artist can ask Muzik2Go to help improve the result.

Possible levels include:

### **Analyze**

Tell me what's happening.

### **Guide**

Tell me how to improve it.

### **Assist**

Help me improve the recording.

### **Polish**

Take the recording further toward a finished demo.

### **Master**

Prepare the completed song for final listening/sharing.

The artist should control how much assistance they want.

---

# **18\. ORIGINAL VS IMPROVED**

Whenever significant AI-assisted improvement occurs, the artist should be able to compare:

**Original**

versus

**Improved**

The purpose is transparency and creative control.

The artist should understand what changed rather than feeling that the AI simply replaced their work.

---

# **19\. MUSIC / INSTRUMENTAL DEVELOPMENT**

Muzik2Go should support artists who have no instrumental as well as artists who already have one.

Artists can explore:

* Musical direction  
* Arrangement  
* Different styles  
* Different moods  
* Different interpretations  
* Music built around their song  
* Music built around their vocal/melody

If the artist already has a beat, Muzik2Go should work around that existing material.

The artist should be able to say:

> "Don't change my song. Just show me what else it could become."

---

# **20\. STUDIO SESSION**

Studio Session combines multiple capabilities into one guided experience.

A session might look like:

### **Step 1 — Prepare**

AI checks what the artist wants to work on.

### **Step 2 — Warm Up**

AI recommends relevant vocal exercises.

### **Step 3 — Practice**

Artist works on the target section.

### **Step 4 — Record**

Artist records several takes.

### **Step 5 — Analyze**

AI identifies strengths and improvement areas.

### **Step 6 — Try Again**

Artist records another take.

### **Step 7 — Compare**

Artist reviews the performances.

### **Step 8 — Develop**

AI helps improve the song if necessary.

### **Step 9 — Save Progress**

The session becomes part of the artist's long-term journey.

The artist can stop at any stage.

---

# **21\. GUIDE ME**

Guide Me is an optional AI experience for artists who don't know what to do next.

It considers the artist's current work and development.

Examples:

> "You have three unfinished songs. Which one would you like to develop?"

> "Your recent practice shows improvement in pitch. Let's challenge that improvement with a song."

> "Your chorus is developed, but your verse is incomplete."

Guide Me should reduce confusion without taking control away from the artist.

# **38\. PRODUCT DIFFERENTIATION**

Muzik2Go should not position itself as simply:

> "An AI recording app."

Its differentiation is the **complete artist journey**.

Most music tools solve one part of the problem.

Muzik2Go aims to connect:

**VOCAL DEVELOPMENT**

↓

**SONG CREATION**

↓

**RECORDING**

↓

**ANALYSIS**

↓

**IMPROVEMENT**

↓

**ARTIST DEVELOPMENT**

The key differentiator is the continuity between those experiences.

The AI remembers the artist's development and uses that understanding to make future sessions more relevant.

---

# **39\. PRODUCT EXPERIENCE PRINCIPLES**

### **Simple for beginners**

A beginner should be able to start without understanding professional studio terminology.

### **Powerful for professionals**

Experienced artists should be able to access deeper controls and workflows when they want them.

### **Artist-first**

AI assists rather than dictates.

### **Preserve creativity**

Experimentation should never require destroying the original.

### **Explain, don't merely score**

Feedback should teach.

### **Progress should be audible**

Artists should be able to hear their development.

### **Everything connects**

Practice should influence coaching.

Coaching should influence development.

Development should influence recording.

Recording should influence analysis.

Analysis should influence future practice.

### **Privacy by choice**

Artists control who sees their work.

**Muzik2Go** is an AI-powered personal music studio for singers and artists.
